Safety first

Built for climbing, jumping and everything in between

Kids don't just use furniture, they test it. So we design every piece with safety in mind from the first sketch.

  • Rounded edges & cornersNo sharp corners at head height. Everything is sanded smooth.
  • Child-safe finishesLow-odour, child-friendly paints and finishes.
  • Fixed to the wallWardrobes, shelves and towers are anchored so they can't tip over.
  • Finger-friendly hardwareSoft-close drawers and hinges, and guard rails on every raised bed.
  • Soft landingsPadded flooring and cushioned zones under climbing and play areas.

FAQ

Questions parents ask

What ages do you design for?

From babies to teens. We'll plan the room for your child's age now, and we can design pieces that adapt as they grow.

Can the furniture grow with my child?

Yes. Adjustable shelves and rails, desks that change height, and beds that convert are all options. Just tell us what you need.

Do you build play areas for businesses?

Yes. We design and build play areas for nurseries, schools, clubs, restaurants, clinics and more. Send us your space and we'll suggest a plan.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size of the project. We'll give you a clear timeline together with your quote.

Is the quote really free?

Yes. Contact us by phone or WhatsApp and we'll prepare a free quote for your project.
