        Safety first

        Built for climbing, jumping and everything in between

        Kids don't just use furniture, they test it. So we design every piece with safety in mind from the first sketch.

        • Rounded edges & cornersNo sharp corners at head height. Everything is sanded smooth.
        • Child-safe finishesLow-odour, child-friendly paints and finishes.
        • Fixed to the wallWardrobes, shelves and towers are anchored so they can't tip over.
        • Soft landingsPadded flooring and cushioned zones under climbing and play areas.

        FAQ

        Questions parents ask

        How do I order a product?

        Add products to your cart and tap Checkout. Enter your delivery details, pay the total with InstaPay, then tap "Send order". Your order opens in WhatsApp, where you attach the payment screenshot and send it to us.

        How much is delivery?

        Delivery is free anywhere in Cairo. Outside Cairo, the fee depends on the product: small items go by courier, while large pieces need a van and our team to install them. Send your order and we'll confirm the fee on WhatsApp before you pay.

        How do I pay?

        We accept InstaPay transfers to our bank account. The total and the account details are shown at checkout. We confirm your order as soon as we receive the payment.

        Can I see the products before buying?

        Yes. You're welcome to visit our showroom in Park Mall, New Cairo. Message us first to check what's on display.

        Can you make a product in a different size or colour?

        Often, yes. Tell us what you need and we'll let you know what's possible.

        Do you build play areas for businesses?

        Yes. We design and build play areas for nurseries, schools, clubs, restaurants, clinics and more. Send us your space and we'll suggest a plan.

        How long does a custom project take?

        It depends on the size of the project. We'll give you a clear timeline together with your quote.
